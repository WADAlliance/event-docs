There are over 100 teams. In order to streamline the process and avoid evaluator fatigue, hubs have nominated exactly 3 top teams. We call this "Team Spotlighting." 

External evaluators then rank those top 3 teams: 1st, 2nd, 3rd. Teams receive points based on their ranking, and the average of all points received, from all evaluator contributions, identifies which is the top team for that hub.
