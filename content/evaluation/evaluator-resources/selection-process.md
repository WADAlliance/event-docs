# What to Review
There are over 100 teams. In order to streamline the process and avoid evaluator fatigue, hubs have nominated exactly 3 top teams. We call this "Team Spotlighting." We are trusting the hubs’ initial selection and keeping this stage intentionally simple.
# Selection Method
External evaluators then rank those top 3 teams: 1st, 2nd, 3rd. Teams receive points based on their ranking. Final scores will be averaged across all evaluator contributions, and the top score identifies which is the top team for that hub. 

| Rank | Points |
| ---- | ------ |
| 1st  | 3      |
| 2nd  | 2      |
| 3rd  | 1      |

Evaluators may consider the [evaluation guidance](/evaluation/evaluation-rubric) which has also been shared with hubs to act as a common reference point in response to the many questions about how projects will be evaluated.

Evaluators will also be asked to provide a brief written rationale for your rankings to support transparency. These responses may also be used to further enrich the globe.

How to submit: [Selection Form](https://tally.so/r/D4kALR).
