What a journey it has been. And yet, this is only the beginning. Over the last weeks, hubs have been organising with local teams to engage with their local contexts from a fresh perspective. Participants have walked their neighbourhoods and identified their own areas they want to work on. Teams have formed around a common purpose. Now, they're starting to take the first steps toward that new potential they're seeing, distilling those insights into a technical build process. Some teams may be aiming for a prototype, some for an MVP (minimum viable product). In all cases, these projects, which are part technological, and part social, may grow to become interventions into their systems of place. Like acupuncture for the body, these teams, in deep relationship with their communities, are unlocking new pathways to walk toward the futures they collectively desire. [^1]

At the beginning we reflected to you your own question, ["How is this hackathon different?"](/resources/hackathonDifference) Now is time to hear from those that felt called to respond. In order to organise your contributions, we will re-open two forms in the same [enrolment app](https://cats.wada.org) for the two primary stakeholder groups of hubs and participants. 

1. **Team Enrolment:** for participants, this is the place to officially register your team. 
	1. Here we will ask you about the purpose that has called your team into action, and your self-defined problem statements. 
	2. This will also be a chance for you to confirm which hub you're related to, if that applies. 
	3. Please only submit your team once! So long as your team has submitted their enrolment, and you are part of that team, you're done. Just select one person from your team to make the submission, or do it all together, but only submit once. Please do not duplicate your team submissions. 
	4. Each team submission will be reflected on [the globe](https://potentialise.wada.org).
2. **Hub Publication:** for hubs, this is the place to submit your [curation](curation). 
	1. This is where the "SEE STORY" button on your hub card on the globe will lead to.
	2. This is the first point of reference for evaluators to start learning about your hub's projects.
	3. Evaluators are short on time. This is the chance to hook them in, generating interest in your projects, and making visible the work happening on the ground.
