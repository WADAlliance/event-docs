# Why 
[CATS](https://cats.wada.org) is more than just a summit. It is about making visible and valuing the work happening on the ground. That happens through evaluation. There is, of course, also awarding of prizes. After all their work, the top teams need to be selected to be invited to Nairobi to present their projects.
