# How will we select hubs? 

[Hubs](/hubs) are selected to participate in the process through evaluation of the following criteria: 

1. Timeliness of completing the [Hub Enrolment Form](https://cats.wada.org/register?type=mD9o55) 
2. Quality of responses, mostly in terms of...
    - Depth of relationship to place
    - Hosting capacity
    - Mission alignment
3. The number and diversity of participant sign-ups associated with your hub
    - Developers
    - Artists
    - Community Organizers
    - Farmers
    - Elders
    - Entrepreneurs
    - Teachers
    - Researchers
    - The list goes on...

To learn more about some of the perspectives which will influence the evaluation of applications, you may review the footnotes section of [prisma.events](https://www.prisma.events), which links several resources that have informed our work.
