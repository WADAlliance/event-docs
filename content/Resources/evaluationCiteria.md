[Evaluation](https://docs.prisma.events/processes/evaluation) is an ongoing process. The criteria will be written in the course of the process. Evaluators will join from multiple disciplines and each brings with them a specialised perspective from which they will evaluate projects. These disciplines include but are not limited to: 

- Regenerative design and development
- Community organising
- Blockchain and AI development

For evaluation to be meaningful for both teams and evaluators, not transactional, it's important for a relationship to be built up over time. 

Reports are static, data lacks qualitative narration, and calls don't scale. The docs and overall publishing stack is there to give hubs a way to create a whole environment with which to engage evaluators, enabling them to see into the work on-the-ground from multiple perspectives. 

For that reason, there are no predefined things to communicate. Teams are encouraged to think not in terms of what is expected of them to communicate but instead how much, to surface their work as a whole system.
