In order for hubs in CATS to be able to reach far and wide, whilst still maintaining a coherent sense of identity, we've prepared the following media assets to use as templates for:

- Social media posts
- Flyers
- Slide decks
- Participant and partner engagements
- Anything else you'd like

You can find the assets on Canva [here](https://www.canva.com/folder/FAF36PpShGE) and they're copied below for convenience. Feel free to download and use as the bases for your designs. 
# Flyer
The flyer template has dimensions 1080x1350 pixels and can be useful for physical printouts to hand to local community members, or as info cards/ resources for use in workshops.
