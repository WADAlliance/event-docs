See also [Prisma Glossary](https://docs.prisma.events/glossary).

**Regenerative Thinking:** A framework that seeks to restore and enhance the vitality of living systems, focusing on relationships, potential, and context rather than problems or scarcity.

**Enactment:** The lived process through which systems and social structures are brought into being through participation and interaction.

**Community of Practice:** A group of people who share a concern or passion and learn how to do it better through regular interaction.

**Living Systems Paradigm:** Understanding the world as composed of self-organizing, adaptive systems that evolve through feedback and relationships.

**Cardano Stack:** The suite of technologies and tools supporting decentralized application development on Cardano, including Plutus, Aiken, Mesh SDK, APIs, and integration points with AI services.

**Potential-Based Design:** A design approach focused on realizing what a system or place can become, rather than fixing what it lacks.

**Publishing Stack:** The collection of documentation, stories, and data produced throughout the hackathon that reflects both technical and social processes.

**Sensemaking:** The collective process of interpreting and giving meaning to experiences to inform action.

**Action Learning:** A cycle of acting, reflecting, and refining understanding based on real-world experimentation.
