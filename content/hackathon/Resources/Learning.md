# Feedback Loop for Continuous Learning

**Cycle:**

1. **Observation:** What is happening in the hub?  
2. **Reflection:** What patterns are emerging?  
3. **Adjustment:** What needs to change in facilitation, pacing, or support?  
4. **Integration:** What have we learned that others can use?

Document feedback weekly and share highlights with Prisma in the Mentorship calls.

# Evaluation of Participant Experience

Use a short reflective survey or discussion at the end of each phase. Sample questions:

* What did you learn about your community or yourself?  
* What moments of collaboration stood out?  
* How has your understanding of regeneration or technology shifted?  
* What support do you need next?

Responses inform real-time improvement and the design of future hackathons.
