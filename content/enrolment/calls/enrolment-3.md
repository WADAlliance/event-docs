# Resources
In this call we covered a quick recap of [last week's enrolment call](/enrolment/calls/enrolment-2), before a very high-level introduction of [timelining](https://docs.prisma.events/processes/process-infrastructuring/timelining). We then learned about [Andamio](https://drive.google.com/file/d/19cbdwD9Jn1NdIoxUSyg2ZkVkXiEXAZRh/view?usp=sharing), the platform is being offered to support your Cardano technical skills learning journey, presented by Darlington. 

We then went into breakout rooms to discuss the question "What does success in this hackathon look like for me and my communities?"
